Model assumptions
Mouse locks onto one empty chair and keeps walking to it until that chair is occupied. If several defenders stand, several chairs can become empty. Once the locked target closes, the Mouse chooses the nearest currently empty chair. Chairs are physical obstacles for everyone. Superman and Orchestrator allow only one intended mover. Circular, Pairs, Zoning and Self-Organization can produce simultaneous responders; they may notice the conflict late, hesitate and retarget to another empty chair. Once a player stands, they must sit on a different chair before they can return to the one they left. People sitting inside the Mouse safety radius do not stand. Human speeds vary by person, repeated running adds mild fatigue, the Orchestrator makes an imperfect visual decision rather than an exact calculation, and Superman launches from a visually estimated halfway point rather than perfect ETA math. Monte Carlo comparisons use the same room layouts for all strategies.
