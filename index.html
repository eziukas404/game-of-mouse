Model assumptions
Mouse locks onto the current empty chair and does not change target until a defender reaches it. A defender's old chair becomes empty when they stand. The Mouse then targets that new vacancy if the defender wins the race. Human delay, communication delay, timing error and the "do not move near a passing Mouse" rule are included. Strategies are deterministic where your rules are deterministic, and stochastic where humans self-organize.
