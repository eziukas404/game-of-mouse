Model assumptions
Mouse locks onto the current empty chair and keeps walking only to that chair. When a defender reaches it first, the defender's old chair becomes the new target. Mouse speed is based on normal walking pace. Player speed is a multiplier of Mouse speed. Human timing is strategy-dependent. A prepared physical response can start in roughly 0.3–0.6 s, while choosing among several people takes longer. The Orchestrator model adds decision time plus verbal/comprehension overhead, so a normal command usually starts movement after roughly 1.4–2.0 s rather than under one second. Chairs are physical obstacles: the Mouse and moving defenders route around them instead of passing through them. Players sitting inside the Mouse safety radius are not allowed to stand.
